Our Story

Developers Community Pokhara started as a small group of friends in the technology industry who met through local meetups in Pokhara. After realizing the broad appeal and clear need for a developer community in the city, the group publicly launched with its first event in 2024.

We are a group of tech enthusiasts grouped together to create a vibrant tech community in Pokhara and build a platform for everyone to learn, share, and grow together. Today, with 1500+ active members, 508 followers on LinkedIn, and 9+ events hosted, we are proud to serve as a thriving hub for Pokhara's software developer community.

Our partnerships with local colleges like Infomax College of IT & Management, the National Innovation Center Nepal, and tech companies like Apify and Datachain.ai mean there's something for everyone — whether you're a developer, student, technologist, or entrepreneur.

Our Mission & Goals

A tech-focused community in Pokhara, Nepal, with an inclusive approach — working together to break barriers and build a thriving tech community.

Language Agnostic

Whatever your language specialization, you're welcome. Being language agnostic lets a diverse range of people come together, share ideas, and learn from each other.

Everyone Welcome

Software developers, tech enthusiasts, students, entrepreneurs — seasoned or just starting out, we welcome everyone in and around Pokhara.

Free Monthly Meetups

Our monthly meetups provide networking, learning, and collaboration for all skill levels — educational, career-opening, and free for everyone.

A Platform to Connect

A place where Pokhara-based developers come together, get to know each other, and exchange ideas — fostering collaboration and mutual growth in the tech field.

What We Stand For

Our values guide everything we do, from the events we host to the partnerships we form.

Community First

We believe in the power of community. Our events bring together developers of all skill levels to learn, share, and grow together in Pokhara.

Accessible to All

Every event is free and open to everyone. We're committed to breaking down barriers and making tech education accessible across Nepal.

Quality Content

From workshops to talks, we focus on delivering high-quality, practical content that helps developers succeed in their careers.
