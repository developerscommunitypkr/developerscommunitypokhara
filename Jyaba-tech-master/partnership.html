Wheeler Games Sponsorship Program

Our newest initiative — companies sponsor event goodies, swags, and prizes for community members.

Sponsor Goodies

Provide T-shirts, stickers, notebooks, or tech accessories for event attendees. Your brand on every item, on every attendee.

Prize Sponsorship

Sponsor prizes for hackathons and competitions — from gift cards to gadgets. Your company name attached to every winner announcement.

Brand Swag

Branded merchandise for the community — badges, bags, or caps. Every item becomes a walking billboard across Pokhara.
